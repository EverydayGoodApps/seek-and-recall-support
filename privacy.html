Privacy policy

Seek & Recall · Last updated: 18 October 2026

Seek & Recall is provided by Ronnie Kanj, trading as EverydayGoodApps, based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how we handle information in version 1.0, on this support website and when you contact us. Contact: everydaygoodapps@gmail.com.

The essentials

Your card library stays on your device. AI is optional and sends the notes you submit to OpenAI through our service, after permission. Accounts and purchase records support your AI credit balance. We do not sell personal information or use your notes for advertising.

Your local library

Categories, decks, sets, cards, study progress and preferences are stored on your device. Manual card creation, CSV import, bulk paste and studying do not send that library to our servers. Version 1.0 does not provide automatic library syncing. Your device or computer backups may contain app data, subject to your backup settings.

Recently Deleted holds removed library items for up to 30 days unless you permanently delete them earlier. Cleanup occurs when the app processes expired items. Notes and details for an unfinished AI request are also saved locally so it can be recovered after interruption.

Apple sign-in and AI credits

Sign in with Apple is used for the optional AI account. We handle Apple’s app-specific identity identifier, a protected fingerprint of it, our account identifier, sign-in verification information, session records and an encrypted Apple token used to maintain or revoke access. The app does not ask us to collect your Apple password, name or email address as part of sign-in.

We store credit balances, a purchase-account identifier, Apple transaction and product identifiers, credit quantities, purchase environment, verification times and refund status. Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment-card details. The server verifies transactions with Apple before adding credits and checks for refunds. Test balances and public purchase balances are separate.

Optional AI processing

When you choose Generate Preview, the notes you submit and your requested card count pass through our Cloudflare-hosted service to the OpenAI API to create flashcards and check content safety. Generated cards are also sent to OpenAI for a safety check before a preview is returned. Automated safety checks can make mistakes and cannot guarantee that every result is appropriate. Notes may contain personal information if you include it. Avoid sensitive personal information or information about others that you are not entitled to share. Recovering an unfinished request may resend its original notes.

Before sending notes, we ask permission and remember your choice on that device. Turn permission off under Settings → AI privacy to require a new permission prompt before another request. Withdrawing permission does not undo processing that has already occurred. The app does not automatically send your entire library to OpenAI.

We request that OpenAI not store the response as a retrievable response object. This is not zero retention: OpenAI may retain prompts, responses and related information for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days by default, with longer retention where required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent harm. Some temporary processing caches may also apply. OpenAI states that API content is not used to train its models by default. See OpenAI’s API data controls and privacy policy.

Our service stores generated previews linked to your account for recovery. They are available for 24 hours after completion, then their contents are removed by scheduled or account-access cleanup, which may run shortly afterwards. We retain request identifiers, a fingerprint of the submitted notes and count, status, credit charges and timestamps to prevent duplicate charges and administer the service. Original notes are processed in transit rather than stored in our application database as plain text. The 24-hour recovery period applies to preview contents in the active database; older copies can remain temporarily in database recovery backups as explained under Retention and deletion.

Young people and AI eligibility

Our current app rules make AI generation and new AI-credit purchases available from age 13 in the United Kingdom and age 16 in the other supported launch countries. These are feature-eligibility rules, separate from the App Store content rating. Users who do not meet the AI rules can still create cards themselves, import cards and study. We do not provide a parental-consent exception for AI below these thresholds.

Before using AI or starting a new credit purchase, you choose the country where you usually live. On supported Apple software, we ask Apple for an age range through its Declared Age Range service. Once your country is known, routine checks take place from the current screen; Apple controls whether a system sharing prompt is required. Our eligibility screen is shown when setup or another action is needed, or when you open it in Settings. The result may be based on information declared by you or your parent or guardian, or confirmed by Apple using another method. We do not request your exact date of birth or identity documents. If age sharing is declined, a check fails, or the returned range does not meet the threshold, the app does not start AI processing or a new purchase. Older software uses an in-app age-range declaration.

Your country choice is remembered on your device. An eligible result is held temporarily in app memory for the signed-in account, for up to five minutes. We do not send your country choice, age range or Apple age declaration to OpenAI or store those values in our application database. You can check again under Settings → AI privacy → Check AI eligibility. Apple manages its age-range records; if they no longer reflect your age, you or your parent can review Apple Account → Personal Information → Age Range for Apps.

AI is intended for factual study material. Do not submit private information about yourself or other people, including names, contact details, medical records or private conversations. We apply content checks to notes and generated cards and provide an AI-content reporting option. These controls reduce risks but do not make AI error-free or replace adult support for a young person.

Security and service operation

Cloudflare provides hosting, database storage and network security. It processes network information such as IP addresses and request details. We use technical logs, usage counters and short-lived rate-limit records to operate the service, investigate failures, prevent abuse and control costs. Our rate-limit database stores a protected IP fingerprint rather than the raw IP address. The application does not intentionally write your study notes into diagnostic logs.

Workers diagnostic logs are currently retained by Cloudflare for three days on our Free plan. This period applies to that logging service, not every Cloudflare security or network record. Other provider records follow the relevant provider’s retention rules.

We do not include advertising or third-party advertising-tracking SDKs. Apple may provide purchase, distribution and diagnostic information under its own services and privacy settings.

Starter-trial records

An eligible Apple identity can receive the starter allowance once. We retain a fingerprint of the app-specific Apple identity and its claim date separately from the account. This record contains no study notes or email address, but it is still treated as personal information because it can recognise a repeat claim. It survives account deletion and has no automatic expiry while the starter offer remains available. We use it only to administer the one-time allowance and prevent repeated claims; contact us about retention or an objection.

Why we use information

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, we rely on performing our agreement with you to provide requested account, generation and credit services; consent for optional sharing of notes with OpenAI; and legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, reliable operation and responding to support requests. Those interests include preventing repeat trial claims and duplicate purchase credits. We also process information where required to comply with law. You can withdraw AI permission without losing manual card creation or studying.

Providers and international processing

Information is shared as needed with Apple for identity and purchases, Cloudflare for hosting and security, OpenAI for requested AI processing, Google/Gmail for support correspondence, and GitHub Pages for this website. These services may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable provider processing agreements describe transfer safeguards such as contractual clauses and relevant adequacy arrangements; contact us for details applicable to your information. Our application database is currently reported by Cloudflare as located in Western Europe, with read replication disabled. It has no jurisdiction restriction configured. This database location does not limit all hosting, technical logs, support access or AI processing to Europe. We do not promise UK-only or EU-only processing.

Provider information: Apple, Cloudflare, OpenAI processing terms, Google, and GitHub.

Reporting AI content

You can open Report AI content from an AI preview or the reporting option shown with an AI error. This prepares an email or shareable reference containing the request identifier and service environment, when available. The app does not automatically attach your notes or generated cards and does not send a report without your action. You choose what additional details to include; remove personal information before sending. Reports go to our support email and are handled as support correspondence. This is not a live or emergency service.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as your message, screenshots or device details. We use it to respond and investigate. We keep correspondence while needed for the request and related follow-up, disputes or legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses and technical request information for security. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or advertising cookies to these pages. Technical log and backup retention is governed by the relevant service provider; contact us if you need further information.

Retention and deletion

Active account, balance, purchase-verification and request metadata are kept while your account is maintained; they do not automatically expire just because you stop using the app. Sessions have limited validity, and temporary verification and rate-limit records expire and are cleaned up. Preview contents follow the recovery period described above.

Cloudflare’s D1 database recovery service currently maintains a rolling history of up to seven days. Older copies of account records and generated previews may remain in that history after removal from the active database, until they fall outside the recovery window. These backups support recovery from accidental loss or corruption; they are separate from the 24-hour preview recovery available in the app.

To delete your account, use Settings → Account → Delete Account. Successful deletion revokes the app’s Apple sign-in access and removes your active server account, sessions, credit account, associated purchase-verification records, request metadata and previews. All remaining credits, including purchased credits, are removed. The trial fingerprint and claim date remain as explained above. Account deletion is not an Apple refund request.

Deleting the account does not delete local cards or all local draft files. Delete unwanted library items and permanently remove them in Recently Deleted; discard unwanted completed AI drafts or use Discard unfinished request for a saved unfinished AI request. Discarding an unfinished request removes its local recovery information; it does not cancel processing already started on the server or refund credits for cards already generated. Removing the app removes its local application files, but Apple or computer backups, Apple’s own transaction records, provider security logs and legally retained support information are managed separately. Backups may retain older copies until their normal replacement or expiry. We cannot recover data held only on your device.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may be entitled to access, correct, delete or obtain a portable copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Email everydaygoodapps@gmail.com to make a request or raise a privacy concern. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity; do not send your password.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or your local data-protection authority. We will update this policy when features or data practices change and revise the date above.
