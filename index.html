How can we help?

For help with flashcards, imports, AI generation or credits, email us. Include your app version, device and a short description of the problem. Screenshots can help, but please remove private information.

Common questions

What can I use for free?

Create cards manually, import CSV files, paste spreadsheet rows, organise your library and study without buying AI credits. Eligible first-time Apple identities receive 10 starter AI credits in the public app, once per identity.

How do AI credits work?

One credit is used per card returned in an AI preview. Credits are reserved while a request runs; unused credits are returned. Failed or empty requests use no card credits. Editing and saving are free. Discarding a generated preview does not refund its credits. Purchased credits do not expire. AI generation is subject to service usage limits and temporary availability.

A purchase completed but my balance did not change.

Check that you are signed into the same Apple account inside Seek & Recall. Open the shopping trolley, tap Refresh, then Check Pending Purchases. Please avoid buying the pack again just to retry. If the balance still looks wrong, contact support with the approximate purchase time and pack size. Refund requests are handled through Apple.

What should I do if AI generation was interrupted?

Open Create with AI and choose Recover Request. Recovery reuses the existing request rather than charging for a second generation. It may resend the original notes. Completed previews are available for recovery for 24 hours; save the cards you want to keep.

Does AI receive my whole card library?

No. The AI feature sends the notes submitted for that generation through our service to OpenAI. We ask permission before sending notes. Change the permission under Settings → AI privacy. Manual imports and studying do not use AI. See our privacy policy for retention and service-provider details.

What format should my import use?

Open Import Cards → CSV File → Download Template or See Example. Front and Back are required; Pronunciation / subtitle and Notes are optional. You can match columns before importing. Use Paste Rows for cells copied from a spreadsheet.

Can I recover deleted cards, sets or decks?

Open Settings → Recently Deleted. Deleted library items can normally be restored for 30 days, unless you permanently delete them sooner. Support cannot recover cards stored only on your device.

Do my cards sync between devices?

Your library and study progress are stored locally; automatic library syncing is not provided in version 1.0. AI credit balances belong to your signed-in account and can be accessed on another device using the same Apple identity. Device backups are managed separately.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the confirmations. You may need to sign in again first. Deletion removes your server account, remaining credits and saved server previews, and revokes the app’s Apple sign-in access. Local cards remain on the device. A protected trial-claim fingerprint is retained to prevent repeated starter allowances. See the deletion section of our privacy policy.

AI can make mistakes. Review generated cards before relying on them, particularly for health, legal, financial or other important decisions.
